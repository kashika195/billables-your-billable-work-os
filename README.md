# Billables: Your Billable Work OS

You are an award-winning SaaS product designer, UX strategist, copywriter, and senior frontend engineer.

Your task is to design and build a premium marketing website for a SaaS product called "Billables."

This is NOT just another corporate B2B website.

The goal is to make visitors instantly understand the product, enjoy exploring it, and book a demo.

Think of the design quality as somewhere between Linear, Stripe, Vercel, Notion, Attio, and Raycast.

The website should feel modern, playful, premium, interactive, and trustworthy.

Avoid generic SaaS templates.

----------------------------------------------------

ABOUT BILLABLES

----------------------------------------------------

Billables is an AI-powered platform built for consulting companies and professional service firms.

It helps companies manage everything related to billable work in one platform.

Instead of tracking work in Excel sheets, emails, WhatsApp messages, and multiple approval systems, Billables centralizes the entire workflow.

The platform includes:

• Time Tracking

• Timesheets

• Project Management

• Client Management

• Reimbursements

• Role Management

• User Management

• AI-powered automation

• CLI integrations

• Mobile App

• Web Platform

Billables helps organizations:

• Track employee work

• Log billable hours

• Approve timesheets

• Approve reimbursements

• Manage projects

• Manage clients

• Generate accurate billing

• Improve visibility

• Reduce manual work

• Eliminate spreadsheets

The audience includes:

• Consulting Firms

• IT Services

• Professional Services

• Agencies

• Accounting Firms

• Legal Firms

• Technology Consulting Companies
